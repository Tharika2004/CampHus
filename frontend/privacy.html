Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

At CampHus, we collect basic information like your Name, College Email, and Preferences to provide a personalized campus experience. This data is stored locally on your device or our secure database.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used to manage event registrations, allow you to post events, and notify you about upcoming campus activities. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, as no system is 100% secure, we encourage users to use strong passwords.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and cookies to remember your theme preferences (Dark/Light mode) and login session.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, feel free to reach out to the CampHus Support Team.
